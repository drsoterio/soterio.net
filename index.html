for science learning

learning scientist/chemist/science communicator

I'm a PhD student in Technology, Media, and Learning at Teachers College, Columbia University, advised by Paulo Blikstein in the Transformative Learning Technologies Lab. I study how the design, interfaces, and algorithmic architectures of physical toolkits, AI, and social networks shape how the public participates in scientific practices.

My work draws on constructionism, critical pedagogy, and sociotechnical perspectives on learning. I'm the inventor of Lab-on-a-Book and a program coordinator at the Paulo Freire Initiative.

Things I'm building_

Tools Competition 2026 winner
lab-on-a-book · inventor

A real chemistry lab, printed on paper.

Lab-on-a-Book is an ultra-low-cost, paper-based science platform that lets students do real experiments, from cyanotypes and chromatography to pH and microbiology, at home or in schools that have no lab. Lab-on-a-Bot, an AI curiosity companion, guides students with questions instead of handing them answers.

  • awardTools Competition winner, Renaissance Philanthropy & The Learning Agency
  • researchConstructionism 2025 and ISLS 2026 papers, including an Outstanding Student Poster Award

paulo freire initiative · program coordinator

Freirean pedagogy, in dialogue with the world.

At Columbia's Institute of Latin American Studies, the Paulo Freire Initiative connects Freirean pedagogy, public dialogue, and educational practice across communities and media. I have been a researcher there since 2024 and a program coordinator since 2025.
