Prep&Plate

The Future of Meal Planning

Prep&Plate is an AI-powered meal planning app designed to simplify healthy eating. By combining personalized recommendations, intuitive design, and seamless grocery integration, Prep&Plate empowers users to plan, shop, and cook meals with ease. Developed by Carolina Sotério, Brandy Coleman, and Elon Perry-Stiner using a user-centered design approach, it addresses the needs of busy individuals and families.

View Demo

How Prep&Plate Was Built

Prep&Plate was developed using a design thinking approach, ensuring it addresses real-world challenges and meets the needs of its users.

View Wireframe

  • Empathize: We conducted user interviews and surveys to understand pain points like time constraints, dietary restrictions, and lack of inspiration.
  • Define: Based on feedback, we framed the app around personalized meal recommendations, grocery integration, and step-by-step cooking instructions.
  • Ideate: We brainstormed features like AI-driven recipe suggestions, meal prep timelines, and grocery delivery integration.
  • Prototype: Wireframes and interactive prototypes were created, incorporating user feedback to refine the design.
  • Test: The app was tested with real users to ensure it was intuitive, efficient, and met their needs.

What Makes It Unique

  • AI-Powered Recommendations: The app uses machine learning to suggest recipes based on user preferences, dietary restrictions, and past behavior.
  • Seamless Grocery Integration: Automatically generate grocery lists and order ingredients directly through the app.
  • User-Centered Design: An intuitive and visually appealing interface makes meal planning enjoyable and stress-free.

What If I Don’t Have an Ingredient?

Prep&Plate’s smart ingredient feature helps users adapt recipes to what they already have at home. If you’re missing an ingredient, the app suggests alternative recipes and shows how many ingredients are missing from your original list. If none are missing, it highlights recipes you can make right away. This feature reduces food waste and makes meal planning more flexible.


Outcomes

Prep&Plate has transformed the way users approach meal planning. By simplifying the process and offering personalized solutions, it has helped users save time, reduce food waste, and maintain healthier eating habits. The app has received positive feedback for its ease of use and innovative features.
